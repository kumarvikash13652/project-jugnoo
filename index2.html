Our Mission

Protecting firefly habitats across every village and town in Bihar

💡

Light Pollution Control

Encouraging people to turn off unnecessary outdoor lights at night to help fireflies mate and glow freely.

🌾

Pesticide-Free Strips

Encouraging farmers to keep small unsprayed boundary strips along fields to keep larvae safe.

🏫

School Awareness

Educating children and youth about environmental protection and local biodiversity.
