Coach Pro-Tip for 4.5 Feet Depth:

An athletic constant depth layout of 4.5 feet is perfect for home-training centers. It contains enough water resistance to eliminate wave rebounce from the pool floor, ensuring clean hydrodynamics for streamline glide practice, while remaining safe enough for a child to comfortably stand up anywhere if exhausted during strenuous endurance drills.

Engineered Pool Layout Structural Guidelines

  • Staircase Optimization: Denoted steps are intentionally limited to a compact 16-inch wide alcove in the corner. This prevents them from encroaching into the 8-foot wide swimming channel, giving her full clearance for arm strokes.
  • Turn Targets: Paint high-contrast vertical black strips exactly on the center points of the starting and turning walls underwater to practice split-second head alignments during fast turns.
  • Overflow Drainage Gutters: Implement high-capacity overflow grills along both longitudinal walls. This dampens backwash ripples and provides flat, calm water mimicry comparable to championship venues.
  • Volume Calculations Check: Confirmed absolute capacity fits standard chemical filtration plants optimized for roughly 83,000+ Liters water loop turn cycles.
